---
title: "0009. License under AGPL-3.0"
description: License under AGPL-3.0.
---

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-12

## Context and problem statement

The project is public and meant to stay open. People could otherwise run a modified copy as a hosted service without sharing their changes.

## Considered options

- MIT or another permissive license.
- GPL-3.0.
- AGPL-3.0.

## Decision outcome

The project is licensed under AGPL-3.0. Dependencies are checked in CI against an allow-list of permissive licenses.

## Consequences

- Good: modified versions, including hosted ones, must publish their source.
- Bad: some organizations avoid AGPL code.
